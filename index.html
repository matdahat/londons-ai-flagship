About Londons.ai

The experience behind Londons.ai.

Matt Meyers

Applied AI strategist, builder and creative technologist

Matt Meyers is an applied AI strategist, builder and creative technologist. Through Londons.ai, a London AI consultancy, he helps companies work out where AI can improve how they operate, create content, develop products and manage knowledge, then helps build it.

He comes to AI as a music, media and technology entrepreneur with more than 30 years in the global music industry. Half English and half Finnish, Matt started at 17 and has built and advised businesses, artists and creative projects across artist development, rights, licensing, production, publishing and promotion.

He has been personally involved in hundreds of recording, licensing and publishing agreements for artists, writers, producers and clients. Those deals led to releases on major and independent labels, more than 100 Top 10 chart successes, dozens of #1s in the UK and abroad, accumulated sales in the hundreds of millions and streams in the billions.

His AI work covers strategy, workflow automation, AI agents, knowledge systems and rapid product development, plus generative video, avatars, imagery, voice, music and AI-powered content production.

Matt loves learning AI and dives deep into each new platform and release as it comes out. He works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and NotebookLM, plus a wide range of automation and development platforms including n8n, Make, Notion, Vercel, Claude Code, Codex and Cowork, and reaches for whichever fits the problem. More than any single tool, what he cares about is getting them to work together: connecting models, automations and business tools into intelligent workflows that help businesses get more done.

His strength is combining creative thinking, commercial experience and technology. He keeps AI practical: finding where it can save time, improve output, bring in new revenue or make new ways of working possible. After a career around recording contracts, publishing, licensing and intellectual property, he is particularly interested in AI that makes commercial and rights sense.

Matt co-founded the music and media company Audiofreaks Music, which works across music production, songwriting, publishing, management, licensing, TV music and marketing campaigns for brands.

Audiofreaks Music has made the title music (theme tunes) for many primetime national TV series, including Ant & Dec's Saturday Night Takeaway on ITV, The Big Reunion on ITV2, Surprise Surprise and Meet the Parents with Holly Willoughby on ITV1, Peter Andre Show on ITV2 and Road to Ibiza on E4. Matt was Music Director and music producer on The Big Reunion, which won at the National Reality TV Awards, and he and his team were also music producers on Netflix's Dance 100.

As an award-winning producer working under several aliases, Matt has had hundreds of chart and club successes, and has produced or worked on projects involving Tiësto, Nicole Scherzinger, Chaka Khan, Alok, Afrojack, CeCe Peniston, Alesha Dixon, Wiz Khalifa, Keri Hilson, Snoop Dogg, Deborah Cox and many others. He also manages the London pop artist Allegra.

In K-pop and J-pop, Audiofreaks Music has worked with TWICE, NCT DREAM, ITZY, IVE, MONSTA X, SUPER JUNIOR, SHINee, TAEMIN, OH MY GIRL, LOONA, YOUNHA, SixTONES, Kis-My-Ft2 and many more.

Across writing, production and remixes, the Audiofreaks Music family has also worked with Mary J. Blige, John Newman, Tom Walker, Mark Morrison, Robin S, Crystal Waters, Martha Wash, Armand Van Helden, Lindsay Lohan, Judge Jules, Blue, Five, Atomic Kitten and Liberty X.

Matt and his business partners created HitVocals, the home of human vocals: an online marketplace where DJs, producers and labels license exclusive acapellas, toplines and hit songs from professional songwriters and vocalists. Discovery, licensing, messaging, rights management and artist payments all happen in one place. They built it with a combination of AI, the no-code platform Bubble, Amazon Web Services and other technologies, and it has become the leading online vocal marketplace in the music industry.

His co-founders include music manager Chris Herbert, whose career includes creating the Spice Girls and later working with Simon Cowell on acts including Five.
